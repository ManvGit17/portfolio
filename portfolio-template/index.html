FIA LEVEL 1 CIRCUIT

Challenge Statement

We have an FIA level 2 circuit, in Bangalore, but had many safety and infrastructure problems. So to encourage racing culture in Bangalore I built an FIA Level 1 (Grade 1) circuit solves the safety, infrastructure, and technical limitations of lower-grade circuits (like Grade 2) primarily through enhanced, specialized infrastructure designed for the high-speed, high-downforce, and advanced technological demands of Formula 1 cars.
